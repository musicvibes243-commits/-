About the studio

A space you want to come back to

Living moss walls, warm timber and soft light — Anikor is built so your body starts to unwind before the practice even begins.

We work without chasing results: in a group the teacher sees everyone and offers a version of each pose that suits your body. Alongside yoga we run sound healing, sadhu board and aromatherapy sessions, and on request the space turns into a venue for a birthday, a hen party or a calm company gathering.

FAQ

The short answers

I have never done yoga. Can I come?

Yes. Come to a trial class for ₽500 — the Morning yoga group, Tuesday or Thursday at 9:00. The practice moves at an unhurried pace and the teacher shows easier versions of every pose. Just say before the class that it is your first time.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes and socks. Mats, blocks and straps are all here, and people practise barefoot.

My back and neck hurt. What should I choose?

Yoga therapy: gentle work with back pain and neck tension, which is often what causes headaches. Tell us about your diagnosis beforehand and the practice will be adapted.

What is sadhu board standing, and does it hurt?

It is the practice of standing on sadhu boards. The guide leads you gently: first your intention and preparation — breathing, warming up the lymphatic and circulatory systems — and only then the board itself. A first stand is usually short.

Can I celebrate a birthday with you?

Yes — birthdays are the thing people most often celebrate here. We also host anniversaries, kids parties, proms, hen parties and company gatherings. The programme is put together around your request.

How do I book?
